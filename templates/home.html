A FEW GOOD QUESTIONS

Before we talk.

What does AutoMake AI actually do?

It helps research businesses, collect public contacts, check email status, prepare and send reviewed outreach, manage configured follow-ups, track replies, and book agreed appointments when calendar availability is confirmed.

Does it send emails without my approval?

Research prepares results and drafts. Sending begins when you choose Send or Activate. After activation, configured follow-ups can continue until replies, opt-outs, or other workflow rules stop them.

Is every email guaranteed to work?

No. Verification can return unknown, risky, or catch-all results, and even a deliverable result does not guarantee delivery or a reply. The app keeps these distinctions visible.

Can I see it with my business in mind?

Yes. Tell us your business type and goal in the demo request, and we can walk through how the workflow fits. We’ll discuss scope and any service or usage costs directly.
